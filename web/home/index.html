Our History

The ALRI GROUP started as a dream in 2020. What began as a conceptual idea from its founder has evolved over the years into a developing ecosystem of specialized technological subdivisions.

Born from a passion for understanding how systems work and how to push their limits, the organization is now transitioning from a personal project to a professional organization.

Corporate Structure

The ALRI GROUP acts as a Holding Company, serving as the parent organization for a set of specialized technological subsidiaries.
